Ad-tech · Programmatic ops

RTB Monster

A programmatic operations cockpit built around one question: where is real margin being made? It reconciles reporting from every ad platform (display, video, CTV, in-app, push) into a single contribution-margin view and turns it into ranked spend actions — automation stays behind trust gates, so the operator approves what matters. Restatement-safe fact tables, per-counterparty Partner P&L, and buyer audits against each DSP's own API keep the numbers honest.

Ad-tech · Exchange infrastructure

Relay

An owned ad exchange — where the other platforms rent pipes, this is the pipe itself. A VAST ad server for CTV, audio and video lanes, an oRTB auction, and a control plane built on one hard seam: config flows down as versioned snapshots the edge polls, counts flow up, and the two planes never share a process — so a dead edge can't take reporting down. The demo is the operator's control plane: publish a config, watch the edge pick it up, screen traffic, follow the money.

Ad-tech · Tracking & attribution

TraQuery

A server-to-server tracking & attribution network designed around a hard constraint: partners need rich reporting without ever seeing each other's economics. So permissions fail closed, pricing is per-geo and per-partner, and postbacks retry until delivered. Fraud handling is built in at the edge — invalid-traffic screening, datacenter detection, incentivised-traffic flagging, bot/test segregation and click-domain failover — because trust in the numbers is the product.

How I work

I start from the operator's day, not a feature list. Every screen in these products began as a concrete workflow problem — reconciling margin across platforms that each report differently, giving partners reporting without exposing the economics behind it, keeping payments honest across currencies. The product decisions are what's worth examining in the demos: what's automated versus approval-gated, what each role can and can't see, and which defaults quietly do the work.

The domain is paid media and monetization — search, social, native, and RTB/programmatic across display, video, CTV, in-app and push. That depth is why the products are opinionated: they encode how the work is actually done, including the standing rule that capital is allocated on true margin after fees, not on platform-reported numbers.

The toolchain is AI-first: Claude Code and agentic workflows are daily production practice — both in how the products are built and in what they do, from email-to-deal parsing to in-product assistants.
